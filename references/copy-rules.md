# Copy Rules

Impact Conversion Resources. Every line of copy this skill produces passes this checklist: upsell pages, headlines, report titles, all of it.

## The register

Write at an eighth-grade reading level or below. Short words over long ones. If the copy feels too simple to work, it's about right. Direct response, not brand poetry: the page has one job, the next click.

## Non-negotiables

### Economy
- Every word earns its place. Cut redundant modifiers ("free gift" becomes "gift"), redundant pairs ("each and every" becomes "each"), and wordy phrases ("in order to" becomes "to", "due to the fact that" becomes "because").
- No hollow intensifiers: very, really, totally, absolutely, unless they carry real meaning.
- Prefer the verb to the nominalization: "recommend", not "make a recommendation".

### Action
- Verbs first, active voice by default. "We tested it on 400 orders", not "it was tested".
- No noun stacks. "A process for identifying candidates", not "a candidate identification process".

### Precision
- The right word, not the fanciest. Specifics over abstractions: a number, a name, a timeframe, a sensory detail. "Smells of fresh oranges" beats "smells great"; "$4.56 per visitor" beats "significant revenue".

### Music
- Vary sentence length and structure. Five same-length sentences in a row is a dead rhythm.
- The punchy fragment works only as contrast after a longer sentence. Build the long line, then drop the short one. Don't fragment everything.
- Put the important word at the end of the sentence.

### Point of view
- Write to "you". Persuasive copy without the reader in it is a brochure.
- Old information first, new information last; each sentence ends where the next begins.

## House style

- **No em dashes.** Use a colon, a comma, or a full stop.
- No tilde for approximations; write "about" or "roughly".
- No invented statistics, testimonials, or credentials, ever. If a claim has no source in the audit, interview, or mining, it doesn't ship.
- Customer quotes appear verbatim or mirrored per the Mirror Rule, never "improved".

## Warning signs to catch in your own drafts

| If you see this | You've violated |
|---|---|
| Five fragments in a row | Music |
| "make a recommendation", "take into consideration" | Action |
| "very", "really" scattered everywhere | Economy |
| "premium quality", "great results" | Precision |
| No "you" anywhere | Point of view |
| A stat you can't trace | The no-invention rule |

## The final pass

Read the copy aloud (or simulate it). Anywhere you stumble, rewrite. Then confirm: purpose clear in the first line, one idea per section, ends on the action you want taken.
